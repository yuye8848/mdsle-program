The MD–SLE shared core program

Methods in brief / 方法概要

Program definition. Direction-consistent differentially expressed genes shared by the MD discovery cohort (GSE202657, 9 vs 9) and the SLE discovery cohort (GSE50772, 61 vs 20; limma, adj. P < 0.05 and |logFC| > 0.585 in both) define the 123-gene shared set; grading by independent-cohort direction replication (T0 = all three layers, 13 genes; T1 = two, 52; T2 = one, 40; T3 = none, 18) and bootstrap stability selection define the locked 65-gene core program (36 up, 29 down).
程序定义：MD 发现队列（GSE202657，9/9）与 SLE 发现队列（GSE50772，61/20）中方向一致的差异基因构成 123 基因共享集（两病均 limma adj. P<0.05 且 |logFC|>0.585）；按独立队列方向复现层数分级（T0 三层 13 个、T1 两层 52、T2 一层 40、T3 零层 18），经自助稳定性选择锁定 65 基因核心程序（上调 36、下调 29）。

Locked-formula scoring. Per-sample ssGSEA scores (GSVA v2) for the up and down arms, computed separately, unchanged in every cohort. Cohort statistic: S = Cliff's δ(up) − Cliff's δ(down) with 10,000 label permutations. Declared input transforms: arrays as deposited; counts as log2(CPM+1); RPKM as log2(x+1).
锁定公式打分：逐样本 ssGSEA（GSVA v2）分别计算上调臂与下调臂，所有队列不作任何改动。队列统计量 S = Cliff's δ（上调）− Cliff's δ（下调），10,000 次标签置换。预先声明的输入变换：芯片用提交值；计数用 log2(CPM+1)；RPKM 用 log2(x+1)。

Evidence layers. Single-cell localisation: donor-level pseudobulk of the CELLxGENE lupus atlas (program strongest in the myeloid/DC axis; cDC per-gene concordance 55/65). Communication: CellChat — no program gene is a ligand/receptor in SLE; NAMPT (VISFATIN) is the sole MD exception. Genetics: disease-level MR null (IVW OR 1.010, 95% CI 0.988–1.032, against the five-biobank MD meta-GWAS GCST90809428; LDSC rg ≈ 0); gene-level MR null for all 55 testable genes; TNFAIP3 co-localises (coloc PP.H4 = 0.977) but SMR is null (p = 0.200) and HEIDI rejects a single shared variant (p = 7.0e-4) — a co-located, heterogeneous locus, not a clean causal signal. Perturbation: CellOracle and scTenifoldKnk on upstream regulators (see target table); Geneformer layer pending.
证据层：单细胞定位——CELLxGENE 狼疮图谱供体级伪 bulk，程序最强于髓系/DC 轴（cDC 逐基因一致 55/65）。细胞通讯——CellChat 显示 SLE 中无程序基因作配体/受体，MD 中仅 NAMPT（VISFATIN）例外。遗传——疾病层 MR 为零（对五库 MD meta-GWAS GCST90809428，IVW OR 1.010 [0.988–1.032]；LDSC rg≈0）；55 个可测基因的基因层 MR 全零；TNFAIP3 共定位成立（PP.H4=0.977）但 SMR 为零（p=0.200）且 HEIDI 拒绝单一共享变异（p=7.0e-4）——共定位但异质的位点，非干净因果信号。扰动——CellOracle 与 scTenifoldKnk 作用于上游调控因子（见靶点表）；Geneformer 层待补。

Reading the numbers. In the evidence matrix, NA means “not tested under the pre-specified design”, never “tested and negative”. Overlap statistics on the Analyze page are descriptive research aids, not diagnostics.
读数规则：证据矩阵中 NA 表示“按预先设计未测”，不是“测了为阴性”。“算”页面的重叠统计是描述性研究辅助，不是诊断工具。
